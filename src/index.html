    03macOS · Utility

    Click-Through

    A macOS utility that removes the extra click it takes to activate a window.

    The problem

    On macOS, the first click on a background window only makes that window active. Say you’re working in one browser window and a second one has a pop-up, or you want to reply in the Messages window. You click once to activate it, then click again to do what you actually meant, like opening a link or selecting an input field.

    Core functionality

    This app removes the need for that first activating click completely. Now the first click activates the window and does the task of that click, such as opening a link or selecting an input field.
